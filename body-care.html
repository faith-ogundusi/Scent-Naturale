THE ART OF SCENT LAYERING

Care for your skin.
Curate your scent.

A beautiful fragrance routine starts with thoughtful body care. Discover how everyday essentials and complementary scents can work together.

YOUR EVERYDAY RITUAL

Build your scent, one layer at a time.

Scent layering is about choosing products that work harmoniously together. You don't need many products or a cloud of fragrance — a few considered steps can create a routine that feels personal.

01

Start with cleansing

Cleanse regularly with a suitable body wash or soap. Pay attention to areas that sweat, and dry your skin thoroughly after bathing.

02

Moisturise thoughtfully

Apply a moisturiser that suits your skin. If you enjoy scented products, choose a fragrance that complements the direction you want to explore.

03

Choose your fragrance

Try a body mist, perfume or fragrance oil according to the product's directions. Start with a light application and adjust to your preference.

04

Layer with balance

Pair scents with complementary notes, or let one fragrance be the star. Test combinations gradually to find what smells best to you.

A GENTLE REMINDER

Freshness comes first. Fragrance comes after.

Fragrance can enhance your personal-care routine, but it doesn't replace bathing, clean clothing or appropriate care for persistent body odour.

Avoid applying perfume or fragranced oils to intimate areas, irritated skin or broken skin. Stop using a product if it causes irritation, and patch-test new skincare products when appropriate.

MAKE IT PERSONAL

Your routine should suit you.

Prefer fresh citrus? Soft florals? Warm, rich notes? Your favourite scent family can guide the products you choose — without needing every product to smell exactly the same.

  • Choose products based on your skin's needs and your scent preferences.
  • Introduce new products one at a time.
  • Check product labels and follow their directions.
  • Keep your routine comfortable, simple and consistent.
